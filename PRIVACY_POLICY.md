# Privacy Policy for Stencil Artist

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

## 1. Overview
Stencil Artist ("the App") is a stencil generation and spray painting visualization application. We prioritize user privacy and data protection.

## 2. Information Collection and Processing
- **No Data Collected:** Stencil Artist does not collect, track, transmit, or share any personal information, usage metrics, or location data.
- **On-Device Processing:** All image manipulation, background segmentation, color separation, and 3D preview rendering take place entirely on your device.
- **No External Servers:** No user images or created stencils are ever uploaded to remote servers.

## 3. Device Permissions
- **Photos / Media / Files:** Required to load local photos for stencil creation and to export generated stencils to your device.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Ads
- The App contains no third-party advertisements, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics SDKs.
